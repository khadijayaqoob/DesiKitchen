We Are DesiKitchen

We are a small team of food lovers based in Lahore, passionate about preserving and sharing authentic Pakistani recipes. Whether you want to submit your grandmother's secret karahi recipe, report a mistake, or just say hello — our inbox is always open.

You can also follow us on social media for daily recipe inspiration, cooking tips, and behind-the-scenes from our kitchen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I submit my own recipe?

Absolutely! Use the contact form and select "Submit a Recipe" as your topic. We review every submission and feature the best ones on the site.

Are all your recipes tested?

Yes — every recipe on DesiKitchen is tested in our home kitchen before being published to make sure the instructions and measurements are accurate.

Can I request a specific recipe?

Of course! Fill in the contact form, select "Recipe Request" and tell us which dish you are looking for. We will do our best to add it soon.

Do you offer cooking classes?

Not yet, but we are planning online cooking sessions soon. Subscribe to our newsletter on the Home page to be the first to know.
